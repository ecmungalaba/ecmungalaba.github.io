About Me

I'm a researcher and data analyst at a public health research institution in Lusaka, Zambia, where I support implementation science research on HIV and other infectious diseases. My work sits where data meets care: I design and manage research data systems, build Python pipelines to clean, link, and organise data from multiple sources, and analyse everything from patients' journeys through care to people's experiences and preferences.

I came to data through physics (BSc) and computer science (PGD), and I still approach problems the way both taught me: start with the system, question the assumptions, then find the signal in the noise. I'm growing towards health data science, and I'm especially interested in how better data and smarter systems can help us ask better questions and make better decisions.

Outside work, I build small apps for the people around me and serve in digital ministry at my local church.
